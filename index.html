🗺️ Places of Power

Iconic Locations

From the sunny shores of Beach City to the cold diamond courts of Homeworld — explore every corner of the universe.

🌊🏖️🌤️🏠⛅
Coastal Town

Beach City

A charming coastal town in Delmarva where Steven lives. Home to the Big Donut, Fish Stew Pizza, Funland Arcade, and the iconic Crystal Temple.

🔮💎🌸⭐🏯
Gem Stronghold

Crystal Gem Temple

The ancient home of the Crystal Gems, carved from a magical giant statue embedded in the cliff above Beach City. Each room reflects a gem's inner world.

🕳️🪨🌋⛰️💀
Ruins

Kindergarten

An abandoned Gem incubation site in the desert where Amethyst was grown. Its facets still drain life from the surrounding land thousands of years later.

🌌💎👑🔷🪐
Gem Homeworld

Homeworld

The authoritarian home planet of Gem civilization, ruled by the four Diamonds. A cold world of strict hierarchy, impossible architecture, and dazzling crystal spires.
